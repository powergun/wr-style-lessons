## A Simple Test for Clarity

You can use this connection between fairy tales and serious writing to test how clear a sentence is likely to seem to readers. Just plug the subject and the verb into this template:

Once upon a time, there was  $ \underline{\text{subject}} $, and one day he/she/it [verb].

If you get a good story, your sentence will probably seem clear:

2b. Once upon a time, there was  $ \underline{\text{Little Red Riding Hood}} $, and one day she was walking...

3b. Once upon a time, there was  $ \underline{\text{the mayor}} $, and one day she supported...
