## 3. A Striking Fact

From 2011 to 2017, the average market value of a ton of recycling dropped from almost $190 to just over $100. This decline was due not only to market forces but also to contamination by nonrecyclable materials.

We can combine all three:

“All the human and animal manure which the world wastes,” observes Victor Hugo in his 1862 novel Les Misérables, “restored to the land instead of being cast into the water, would suffice to nourish the world.” Although Hugo was writing in the mid-nineteenth century, he captures the impulse that motivates today’s recycling movement.
