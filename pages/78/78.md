## Here's the Point

Before you begin writing, name the things you are writing about. Those are your topics. They should be short, concrete, familiar words, and more often than not, they should name the main characters in your story. Most of your subjects should be topics. Be consistent: do not vary your subjects merely for the sake of variety. Together, your subjects should name the topics that tell your readers what a passage as a whole is about.
