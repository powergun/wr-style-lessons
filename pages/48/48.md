## A Qualification: Useful Nominalizations

We have so relentlessly urged you to turn nominalizations into verbs that you might think you should never use them. But in fact, you can’t write well without them. The trick is to know which to keep and which to revise. Keep these (verbs are boldfaced, nominations are capitalized):

✓ These ARGUMENTS all depend on a single unproven claim.

1. A nominalization that is a short subject that refers to a previous sentence:

✓ This DECISION can lead to positive outcomes.
